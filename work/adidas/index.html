Adidas Training by Runtastic mobile app development

Adidas Training by Runtastic app for the Facebook Portal TV device.

A workout app that has to work from the sofa, across the room, on a TV-sized screen and with nothing but a remote or voice. Adidas Training by Runtastic on Facebook Portal TV was an exercise in designing for a device nobody holds.

About the brand

Runtastic is the fitness app brand from Adidas, now sold as the adidas Running and adidas Training apps. They track runs, walks, hikes and cycling and offer workouts, and logged activities earn adiClub points. Adidas is a German sportswear company headquartered in Herzogenaurach. Source: Wikipedia. Source: adidas Running on Google Play.

www.runtastic.com

My role

I built the Adidas Training by Runtastic app for the Facebook Portal TV device.

What I built

  • Ported the Adidas Training Android app to Android TV on Facebook Portal TV as the sole developer, in partnership with Adidas and Facebook.
  • Built video playback and download, guided timed workouts and training plans personalised to the user's body data, with fitness tracking and rewards.

Architecture and approach

  • Android TV UI built for the ten-foot experience: focus handling, large type and remote or voice navigation.
  • Video playback and offline download of workout content.
  • A workout engine for guided, timed sessions that drives video, timers and tracking together.
  • Sole-developer delivery: architecture, build pipeline and release owned end to end.

Hard problems

Designing for focus, not touch

Android TV has no touch. Every screen needed predictable focus order, so a person can start a workout with a remote or voice from across a room.

Reusing a phone app on a TV

The mobile app's logic had to be adapted to a different input model, screen size and device, without forking the product.

Outcome

  • Delivered in four months, November 2020 to March 2021.
  • Shipped on a new smart-display TV device for Adidas and Facebook.
