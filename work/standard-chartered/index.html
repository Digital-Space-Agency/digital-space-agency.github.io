Shoal for Standard Chartered mobile app development

Shoal, a sustainable savings app built with Standard Chartered: savings pots backed by a green and social project portfolio.

A savings app has to feel simple while handling account opening, identity and money movement. Shoal added a second challenge: a short engagement and a release date that did not move.

About the brand

Shoal is a mobile savings app where people earn interest through Savings Pots while their money is referenced against a global portfolio of green and social projects. The sustainable asset portfolio is managed by Standard Chartered, and the app shows the CO2e avoided and clean water generated by each saver's pots. Source: Wikipedia. Source: Shoal.

www.shoalapp.com/

My role

I worked on the Shoal app for Standard Chartered, in a regulated financial product where testing and release discipline matter.

What I built

  • Delivered Shoal for Standard Chartered Ventures, through Hi Mum! Said Dad, as the solo Flutter developer, taking the app over the line to release in a short engagement (January 2022).
  • Built onboarding, account setup and deposit features, with AWS Cognito authentication.

Architecture and approach

  • Flutter and Dart cross-platform app for iOS and Android.
  • GraphQL for account and banking operations.
  • AWS Cognito for sign-up, sign-in and session handling.
  • Automated testing and CI/CD from the start, so release candidates could be cut with confidence.

Hard problems

Regulated flows with no room for guesswork

Onboarding and deposits touch real money, so each step needed explicit error and empty states, tested before release rather than discovered after.

Getting to release quickly

As the only Flutter developer, the priority was a shippable vertical slice: authentication, account setup and deposits first, polish second.

Outcome

  • Shoal released: a savings app where Savings Pots are referenced against green and social projects.
  • Delivered as a solo Flutter developer in a short engagement.
