About the brand

The Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS F1 Team app is the team's official mobile app for fans, covering news, analysis, photos and video from inside the Formula One team. The team is based in Brackley, England, and won eight consecutive Constructors' Championships from 2014 to 2021. Source: Wikipedia.
