BBC Good Food mobile app development

Recipe app project.

Recipe apps live in the kitchen: wet hands, a propped-up phone and a timer going. BBC Good Food's app is judged by whether it stays out of your way while you cook.

About the brand

The Good Food: Recipe Finder app (formerly the BBC Good Food app) is an ad-free cooking app with thousands of kitchen-tested recipes, exclusive meal plans, a cook mode and how-to videos. It is separate from the Good Food Magazine app, which is the digital edition of the print magazine. Source: Wikipedia. Source: Good Food app FAQ.
