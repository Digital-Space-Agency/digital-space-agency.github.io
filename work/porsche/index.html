Porsche mobile app development

Mobile app development for Porsche.

Unlocking a car from a phone is a small action with a large trust problem. Porsche Car Connect was one of the early connected-car apps, in the years when remote services were new.

About the brand

Porsche Car Connect is the connected-car service for Porsche vehicles, used through the Porsche mobile app. Its Remote, Safety and Security services let owners lock and unlock the car, control the climate, manage electric charging and see vehicle data such as mileage, tyre pressure and range. Source: Wikipedia. Source: Porsche Car Connect services.
