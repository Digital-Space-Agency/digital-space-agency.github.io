Canon Lifecake mobile app development

Lifecake, the family photo and video app owned by Canon.

Parents take thousands of photos of their children and never look at them again. Lifecake turned the pile into a private, shareable timeline, and Canon bought it.

About the brand

Lifecake was a private family photo and video app that turned a child's photos into a timeline for sharing with family. It was started in London in 2012 and acquired by Canon Europe in 2015. The service closed on 30 June 2020, and its founders launched BackThen as a successor. Source: Wikipedia. Source: Lifecake timeline.
