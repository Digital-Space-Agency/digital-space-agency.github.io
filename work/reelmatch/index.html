ReelMatch

Swipe movie trailers, match with friends and decide what to watch. Flutter, Firebase, TMDB and RevenueCat. Live on the App Store and Google Play.

Two people on a sofa, forty minutes of scrolling, and still no film. ReelMatch turns that into a swipe: each person likes what they like, and the app finds what they both want to watch.

What it is

ReelMatch is my own app for deciding what to watch together. You swipe through recommendations in a Tinder-style stack, watch trailers, and match with friends on shared interests.

My role

I designed, built and run ReelMatch end to end, live on the App Store and Google Play.

What I built

  • Designed, built and run end to end: product, UX, architecture, backend, builds and release on the App Store and Google Play.
  • A Tinder-style swipe stack of recommendations with trailers, a friends list, shared likes and a match view that shows what two people both want to see.
  • A free tier and a premium tier with in-app purchases, invitations with referral rewards, and push notifications.

Architecture and approach

  • Layered architecture: screens and widgets, then providers (ChangeNotifier), then repositories, then services and APIs (Firebase, TMDB, RevenueCat).
  • Provider for state management and GetIt for dependency injection, with everything registered in one locator.
  • GoRouter for declarative navigation, with an analytics observer on every route.
  • Firebase Auth and Firestore for users, likes, friends and recommendations; Remote Config for feature flags; Cloud Messaging for notifications.
  • Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring in production, plus a custom analytics event schema.
  • A subscription layer on RevenueCat, with plan limits enforced by a SubscriptionGuard widget and swipe-limit service.
  • Unit and widget tests mirrored against the lib/ structure with Mockito.

Hard problems

Recommending to users with no history

Cold-start: before a user has ten likes the engine blends popular and genre-based titles. After that it mixes TMDB recommendations seeded from their likes, filtered by genre, language and streaming platform, retries on API failure and de-duplicates across sources.

A production crash in a dependency

Crashlytics showed a fatal AnimationController double-dispose when a swipe was cancelled. I traced it to the swiper package, forked it into the repo with an _isDisposed guard and registered the fix as a dependency override.

Image memory on older phones

A swipe stack is image-heavy. The image cache is capped (200 images, 50 MB), image errors are suppressed and logged separately, and loading goes through a single cached network image widget.

Free vs premium without leaks

Limits are enforced centrally rather than per screen, so a new feature cannot accidentally bypass the paywall.

Outcome

  • 14,000+ downloads across the App Store and Google Play.
  • Live on both stores with subscriptions, invitations and review-prompt logic.
  • Continuous delivery through GitHub Actions and Xcode Cloud.
