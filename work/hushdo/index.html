Hushdo

Voice notes that are transcribed, structured and searched entirely on-device with local AI models. Zero server uploads. Early access waitlist open at hushdo.com.

Voice notes are the most private data on your phone, and almost every voice app sends them to a server. Hushdo does the opposite: speech recognition and the language model both run on the device.

What it is

Hushdo is my own voice-notes app. Notes are transcribed, structured and searched entirely on the device using local AI models, with zero server uploads. An early-access waitlist is open at hushdo.com.

hushdo.com

My role

I am designing and building Hushdo as a privacy-first product.

What I built

  • Designing and building Hushdo end to end: a private, on-device voice-to-tasks app, iOS first and Android second.
  • Whisper speech-to-text and small quantised language models run locally, so recordings and text never leave the phone.

Architecture and approach

  • Flutter app generated with Very Good CLI: flavors (development, staging, production), strict lints, localisation and a test setup from day one.
  • On-device speech recognition with Whisper through sherpa_onnx, and local GGUF language models through llamadart.
  • Models are downloaded at runtime, never bundled, through a signed manifest in a separate repository, so the app stays small and updates are verified.
  • A benchmark harness with a pass bar: only profile and release numbers on real phones count for latency, memory and quality.
  • iOS 16.4+ and macOS 14+ targets.

Hard problems

Fitting a language model on a phone

Choosing a model size and quantisation that is accurate enough to extract tasks from speech and still fast, cool and memory-friendly on real devices. A throwaway spike screen measures this before any feature is built.

Trustworthy model delivery

Models are large and downloaded at runtime. A signed manifest means the app only installs files it can verify.

Outcome

  • Early-access waitlist open at hushdo.com.
  • Website and model hosting in separate repositories.
