Accedo NUVU mobile app development

Nuvu, Ericsson's video-on-demand service for Africa, built as architect and developer through Accedo in Stockholm.

In 2015 most of sub-Saharan Africa could not stream video reliably or cheaply. Nuvu, Ericsson's subscription video service, was designed around that: download overnight when mobile data is cheap, watch offline later.

About the brand

Nuvu was Ericsson's first end-to-end subscription video-on-demand service, announced in November 2015 for Africa and marketed with mobile operators, starting with Airtel Nigeria. Subscribers got around 3,000 local and international titles for a small monthly fee, downloaded at off-peak times and watched offline. Accedo is a global provider of streaming software and services founded in Stockholm. Source: About Accedo. Source: Ericsson launches NuVu, Nov 2015. Source: Nuvu brand and UI design.

What I built

  • Architect and developer on Nuvu by Ericsson, working for Accedo (2015 to 2016), the agency in a fully agile team at Accedo's Stockholm office.
  • Nuvu launched as an Android app with a personalised interface, bookmarking and recommendations, and was marketed with operators, starting with Airtel Nigeria.

Architecture and approach

  • Nuvu's core design: content downloads are scheduled into off-peak network windows on an Android client.
  • Offline playback: downloaded titles stay watchable for up to 30 days.
  • Integration with the operator's customer relationship management and payment systems, so billing goes through the carrier.
  • A content catalogue of around 3,000 local and international films and series for a small monthly fee.

Hard problems

Making expensive data cheap

The service downloads content at off-peak times at no additional data cost, and also supports Wi-Fi. This only works if downloads survive unreliable cellular networks.

Operator billing

Subscriptions had to plug into the carrier's CRM and payment stack rather than a card form, which shapes sign-up, entitlement and renewal.

Outcome

  • Public launch announced by Ericsson in November 2015, with Airtel Nigeria, a network of more than 30 million subscribers.
  • Brand identity by Red Bee, an Ericsson company; bespoke UI and design system by PS Fennell.
