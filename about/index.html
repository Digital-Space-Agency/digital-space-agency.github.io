About David Miller

Lead Flutter developer in Hamburg with 15 years in mobile and 7 shipping Flutter. Now also building AI agents and n8n automations with the same release discipline.

I have shipped production apps for FIFA, Porsche, Adidas, Canon, F1 TV, OneFootball and Standard Chartered, worked with IBM on an Android app for the Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS F1 Team and with Accedo in Stockholm, and I build my own products, including ReelMatch, Hushdo and the open-source FlutterFix, plus n8n and AI agent automations. I studied at Northumbria University and am completing WBS Coding School's AI Agents programme.

How the agency works

Digital Space Agency began as a partnership. It is now just me, supported by AI agents and n8n automations that run the back office and speed up engineering. You always deal directly with the person doing the work, and every release is reviewed and tested by me.
